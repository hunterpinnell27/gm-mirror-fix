GM Mirror Fix launched in 2016 to fix the broken power-fold (retraction) feature on GM900-series trucks and SUVs — Tahoe, Suburban, Yukon, Denali, Sierra, and Silverado. The culprit in almost every case is a small plastic spur gear inside the mirror's motor housing that cracks or strips over time. Our redesigned, strengthened gear — metal where the original GM part used plastic — fixes it for good. See the full story of how this started, or how the repair works.
