GMC Yukon & Yukon Denali Power-Fold Mirror Repair

2007-2014 Yukon and Yukon Denali (GMT900 generation), including the extended Yukon XL.

Denali is GMC's premium trim badge on top of the standard Yukon — it doesn't change the mirror hardware. Whether you drive a base Yukon SLE or a loaded Denali, the power-fold mirror assembly is the same part shared across GM's GMT900-generation SUVs (Tahoe, Suburban, Yukon, Escalade). The failure point is the same too: a small plastic spur gear inside the motor housing cracks or strips over time, and the mirror stops folding.

Model years: 2007-2014 Trims: SLE, SLT, Denali Includes Yukon XL

What we fix on the Yukon / Yukon Denali

  • Mirror won't fold in or out (broken spur gear — most common cause)
  • Mirror makes a clicking or grinding sound (see clicking noise and motor grinding)
  • Mirror glass rattles or vibrates at speed

Full process: remove the mirror using our removal instructions, ship it to us, and we repair and return it — pricing starts at $279 for our gear + motor repair service, 3-day turnaround, 1-year warranty.
