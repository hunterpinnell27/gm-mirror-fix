Chevrolet Suburban Power-Fold Mirror Repair

2007-2014 Suburban (GMT900 generation) — same power-fold mirror design as the Tahoe, just on the extended-wheelbase body.

The Suburban is essentially a Tahoe on a longer frame, and GM used the same power-fold mirror assembly across both. So the failure is identical: a small plastic spur gear inside the mirror's motor housing wears down or cracks over time, and the mirror stops folding in and out — or starts making noise without actually moving.

Model years: 2007-2014 Trims: LS, LT, LTZ Platform: GMT900

What we fix on the Suburban

  • Mirror won't fold in or out (broken spur gear — most common cause)
  • Mirror makes a clicking or grinding sound (see clicking noise and motor grinding)
  • Mirror glass vibrates or feels loose at highway speed

Full process: remove the mirror using our removal instructions, ship it to us, and we repair and return it — pricing starts at $169 for gear replacement, 3-day turnaround, 1-year warranty.
