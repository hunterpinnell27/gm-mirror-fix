Cadillac Escalade Power-Fold Mirror Repair

2007-2014 Escalade and Escalade ESV (GMT900 generation) — the vehicle that started this whole business.

This is the exact problem GM Mirror Fix was built to solve: our founder's wife's 2007 Escalade had its power-fold mirror quit, and the Cadillac dealership quoted almost $1,000 for a full replacement mirror. The actual failure turned out to be a single small plastic gear — read the full story. The Escalade shares its mirror assembly with the Tahoe, Suburban, and Yukon/Denali of the same generation, so the same $40 part and the same repair fix it.

Model years: 2007-2014 Includes Escalade ESV Platform: GMT900

What we fix on the Escalade

  • Mirror won't fold in or out (broken spur gear — most common cause)
  • Mirror makes a clicking or grinding sound (see clicking noise and motor grinding)
  • Mirror glass blurs or vibrates at highway speed

See what a real customer's experience looked like on our testimonials page. Full process: remove the mirror using our removal instructions, ship it to us, and we repair and return it — pricing starts at $169 for gear replacement, 3-day turnaround, 1-year warranty.
