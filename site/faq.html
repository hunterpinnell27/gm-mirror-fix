Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the repair take?
3 days from when we receive your mirror, before we ship it back to you.
Is there a warranty?
Yes — every repair carries a 1-year warranty.
Do I need to be near McKinney, TX?
No. GM Mirror Fix is a mail-in repair service — you don't need to live nearby. You remove the mirror yourself (see our removal instructions), ship it to us, and we ship it back once it's repaired.
What's broken — the gear or the motor?
About 95% of the time it's a broken plastic spur gear inside the motor housing. In the remaining cases, the small electric drive motor itself has failed or lost torque. See Repairs for how to tell, or just order the combo service if you'd rather not diagnose it yourself.
How does payment work?
A $50 deposit is collected at order time, which is fully credited toward your total. The remaining balance is due once your repair is complete, before we ship it back to you.
What if my mirror turns out to be unrepairable?
Your deposit is fully refunded — either if the unit turns out unrepairable, or if you cancel before shipping your mirror to us.
Which vehicles does this cover?
2007-2014 GM900-series trucks and SUVs: Chevrolet Tahoe, Suburban, and Silverado; GMC Yukon, Yukon Denali, and Sierra; Cadillac Escalade.
Can I just buy the gear and do it myself?
Yes — the redesigned spur gear is available on its own for $40 ($75 for a pair) if you'd rather install it yourself. See pricing.
