Start a Repair Order

Four steps: choose your service, pay the deposit, remove and ship your mirror, we repair and return it.

  1. Choose your service — spur gear, motor, or combo (gear + motor). See pricing.
  2. Pay a $50 deposit to reserve your order and get your order number (RMA). Deposit is credited toward your total and fully refunded if your mirror turns out unrepairable or you cancel before shipping it to us.
  3. Remove and ship your mirror — see removal instructions and packing guidance (emailed with your order number).
  4. We repair and ship it back — 3-day turnaround from receipt. You'll pay the remaining balance once the repair is done, before we ship it back.
