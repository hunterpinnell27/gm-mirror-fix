Current Offerings

Customer removes the mirror and ships it to our shop. Every service includes a full mail-in repair: we disassemble the mirror, open the spring-loaded housing, install the new part(s), then lubricate and reseal the entire unit before shipping it back. Turnaround is 3 days from receipt, and every repair carries a 1-year warranty.

How payment works

A $50 deposit is collected at order time — see the order page for details and your shipping instructions. The balance is due once your repair is complete, before we ship it back. Deposit is fully refunded if your mirror turns out unrepairable or you cancel before shipping it to us.
